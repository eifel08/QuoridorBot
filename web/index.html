Quoridor

10 walls · goal: top row
10 walls · goal: bottom row

How to play
  • Be the first to get your pawn to the tinted row on the far side: red goes to the top, blue to the bottom.
  • Move: tap your pawn to see grey dots on the legal squares, then tap one. You can also tap a legal square directly or drag your pawn onto it. If the other pawn is in the way you can jump over it, or diagonally past it when a wall or the edge blocks the jump.
  • Wall: drag a wall from your reserve onto a groove. Over a horizontal groove it covers the square under your finger and the one to its left; over a vertical groove, that square and the one below. Green means legal, red means not.
  • Walls can't overlap or cross, and can't completely cut either player off from their goal.
  • Esc or right-click cancels a drag. Ctrl+Z undoes.
